# PUPI product and business vision

## A useful companion in the household

PUPI explores a family companion that combines emotional conversation with useful daily routines.
The product question is whether companionship becomes more valuable when it also helps people
organize medication information, understand daily data, and keep family members connected.

The current software demonstrates interfaces and development logic. The commercial propositions
below are hypotheses for partner and family validation.

## Three reasons a household might return

| Household need | Product response | Value to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An older adult wants company and an easy way to express a feeling | Approachable conversation, voice/text entry, relaxation shortcuts, and daily activities | Whether people choose to return and find the dialogue useful |
| Medication information is difficult to keep organized | Home medicine cabinet, stock display, time slots, medication calendar, and local confirmations | Whether families find the routine clearer and easier to coordinate |
| Health information is difficult to interpret or share | Readable overview, detailed data views, and a proposed authorized family summary | Whether the information supports better family conversations without confusion |

These are intended user benefits, not measured outcomes. The current health values are simulated.
Medication records do not prove that a dose was taken or that a device dispensed it.

## User, buyer, and channel

**Primary user:** an older adult seeking an approachable companion and simple daily support.

**Potential buyer:** the older adult or an adult child who values companionship and household coordination.

**Potential channel:** companion-device companies and senior-service organizations that can help
introduce, set up, and support the service. A partner relationship would require a defined division
of software, hardware, data, support, and commercial responsibilities.

## Revenue options to validate

- **Software licensing with a hardware partner.** The companion device supplies a physical presence,
  while PUPI contributes dialogue, household workflows, and information design.
- **Optional family subscription.** Explore personalized content and authorized summaries only after
  user demand, operating cost, and privacy expectations are understood.
- **A defined pilot or integration engagement.** Test a specific household scenario before assuming
  a broad consumer launch or institutional sales model.

No prices, revenue projections, orders, or existing commercial agreements are asserted.

## What makes the proposed offer coherent

Conversation offers an emotional reason to engage. Medication organization and everyday information
offer practical reasons to return. A physical companion could make the service easier to access
for people who prefer speaking to navigating a phone.

The combination is the product thesis. It still needs evidence that the different functions
belong together for a clearly defined household, rather than simply adding more features.

## Pilot questions before expansion

| Decision | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- |
| Does the household find it useful? | Usability observations, voluntary repeat use, and reasons for stopping |
| Is there a commercial buyer? | Buyer interviews and clearly scoped willingness-to-pay research |
| Can the service support its cost? | Model/voice usage, hosting, setup and support effort, hardware/partner costs |
| Are medication and data views understandable? | Data-entry accuracy, timing-rule tests, comprehension, and confusion reports |
| Does the physical interface help? | Device reliability, speech-pipeline tests, and comparison with phone-only use |

Predictive analytics can be explored after the project has reliable, appropriately authorized data
and a defined prediction task. The present demo does not establish forecasting accuracy,
emotion-recognition performance, clinical effectiveness, or autonomous medical decision-making.

## Collaboration fit

PUPI can be presented as a software and product-development starting point for emotional-companion
robots. A proposed collaboration could connect dialogue and information systems with a hardware
partner's device, then evaluate one household use case and its commercial viability.

Any collaboration, technical role, deployment plan, or commercial agreement should be described
at its actual stage. This showcase does not announce a formal university or company partnership.
